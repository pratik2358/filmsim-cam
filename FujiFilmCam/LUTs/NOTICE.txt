Changes: original level-12 sRGB Hald CLUT PNGs were resampled with trilinear
interpolation to 33x33x33 samples and written as raw little-endian UInt16 RGB
(red fastest, then green, then blue). These adapted files are distributed under
CC BY-SA 4.0. They approximate film-stock colour only (no grain/halation).
The trademarked names describe the intended look; no affiliation or endorsement.

Camera looks (cam-*.lut) are NOT from the RawTherapee collection. They were fitted by this
project (tools/fit-camera-looks.py) to measure how each Fujifilm film simulation changes colour
relative to PROVIA, using Fujifilm's publicly shown sample photos (same RAW rendered in each
simulation). No images are included in this project. They are approximations, not Fujifilm data.
