FilmCam

Every look is a 3D colour lookup table applied on your GPU to each camera frame, and to the full-resolution photo when you press the shutter. Nothing is uploaded: the camera feed and your photos never leave your device.

Camera looks

Velvia, Astia, Classic Chrome, Classic Neg., Nostalgic Neg., Reala Ace, Pro Neg. Std/Hi, Eterna, Bleach Bypass and Acros are colour LUTs fitted to Fujifilm's published sample photos, measuring how each simulation changes colour relative to Provia. Provia is a mild built-in recipe. They capture colour and tone only (no grain or sharpening) and are approximations, not Fujifilm's own processing.
