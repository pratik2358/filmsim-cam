FilmCam

Every look is a 3D colour lookup table applied on your GPU to each camera frame, and to the full-resolution photo when you press the shutter. Nothing is uploaded: the camera feed and your photos never leave your device.

Camera looks

Provia, Velvia, Astia, Classic Chrome, Classic Neg., Nostalgic Neg., Reala Ace, Pro Neg., Eterna, Bleach Bypass and Acros are hand-tuned approximations built from colour recipes (tone curves, per-hue hue/saturation/lightness, split toning). They are not Fujifilm's own processing.
